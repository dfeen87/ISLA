# Creator Listening Guide

Listening acceptance and preferences are pending. The showcase includes a
Bypass reference, each original mode, and each preselected experimental center.
An additional low-depth Melt .15 comparison is labeled as a post-screen option.
Legato and center Melt include unresolved attack-screen failures in 44.1 kHz
recording tests; their samples are diagnostic. No sample is labeled better.

Open the listening showcase, choose a passage and profile, and keep your playback
volume fixed. Start comfortably low. Normal passages share an integrated
loudness target at or below −23 LUFS. Near-silence and silence retain fixed unity
gain; do not increase volume to chase them. The source dynamics remain intact.

First listen without consulting the measured-difference rankings. For each pair,
write down three independent answers:

1. Can you hear a repeatable difference: yes, uncertain, or no?
2. Which do you prefer for this passage: original, experiment, or no preference?
3. What changed in your own words? A mode name need not fit your description.

Use the optional hidden A/B labels to reduce expectation. Reveal identities only
after noting an impression. This is a listening aid, not a scored ABX experiment.
Repeated listening or an informal small trial does not establish population
audibility. Leave room for a useful result that differs across material.

| Passage | Neutral observations to make |
|---|---|
| Recorded piano and generated piano-like attacks | Timing, edge character, decay, note separation; any change you find useful or distracting |
| Connected melody | Separation and continuity between notes; clarity of the rhythm |
| Sustained harmonics | Tone stability, modulation and changes across the sustain |
| Percussion | Hit definition, spacing, any new ringing or softened edges |
| Dense mixture | Ability to follow parts and overall balance |
| Stereo partial probe | Position, width and stability with headphones/speakers you normally use |
| Gapped tone | The boundaries and center of the gap; consistency of the returning tone |
| Near-silence and silence | Unexpected sound or unstable tails at the same fixed listening volume |

The player uses one audio element. Switching versions keeps the intended play
position but reloads a file; a brief browser gap is a presentation limitation.
Restart both versions to compare attacks. Do not interpret a file-switch sound
as a LIVE mode-transition result. Mode switching was validated separately in
software. Playback happens only when you choose to start it.

The generated passages are 6.0 seconds. The recorded piano is original seconds
2–8 plus .125 seconds of silence per end, totaling 6.25 seconds. All listening
files are 48 kHz stereo. Normal files use PCM24 FLAC; below-gate controls use
FLOAT WAV to preserve near-silence. No limiter, dynamic normalizer, crop fade
or undocumented edit is applied. Provenance includes gains, loudness, hashes,
quantization error and sample/4× peak probes for every version.

After listening, note the passage, profile, amount, answer, confidence and any
timestamps. A preference can be conditional on source material. Any candidate
without a useful repeatable distinction can be discarded. Your observations
are the next input; no owner preference has been assumed or recorded here.
